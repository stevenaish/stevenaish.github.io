      <h1>Guidewire Marketplace accelerators</h1>
      <p class="positioning">At Cognizant, I have led teams that published eleven accelerators to the Guidewire Marketplace.</p>
      <p class="measure">My role included strategy, direction, approach, and the relationship with Guidewire. The teams developed the accelerators. Nine of the eleven are insurance product models for PolicyCenter, provided as Advanced Product Designer mind maps and XML templates that insurers can use to configure a line of business. The other two are platform and data tools. All eleven are listed for Guidewire Cloud Platform.</p>
      <p class="measure">They include Canadian commercial auto product models (Excess Auto, Non-Owned Auto, and Contingent Lessor).</p>

    <h2 id="acc-models">Insurance product models</h2>
    <p class="text-secondary measure">Each product model packages coverages, limits, deductibles, terms, and data elements for one line of business as Advanced Product Designer mind maps and XML templates for PolicyCenter.</p>
    <h3 class="group-label">Canadian commercial auto product models</h3>
    <ul class="card-grid card-grid--3">
      <li class="card card--listing">
        <h4>Canada Excess Auto Product Model</h4>
        <p>A product model for excess auto insurance in Canada. It covers bodily injury and property damage liability with limits and deductibles for Canadian provinces, mandatory and additional coverages, coverage terms, and a framework for the product's data elements.</p>
        <p class="works">Works with PolicyCenter and Product Models (APD)</p>
        <a class="link-more link-external" href="https://marketplace.guidewire.com/product/canada-excess-auto-product-model-by-cognizant/01tPW00000BuVxFYAV">Official listing<span class="visually-hidden"> (opens external site)</span></a>
      </li>
      <li class="card card--listing">
        <h4>Canada Non-Owned Auto Product Model</h4>
        <p>A product model for non-owned (hired) auto insurance in Canada, the liability cover for businesses when employees use vehicles the business does not own. It covers third-party liability with limits and deductibles for Canadian provinces, additional coverages, standard conditions, mandatory coverages, and a framework for the product's data elements.</p>
        <p class="works">Works with PolicyCenter and Product Models (APD)</p>
        <a class="link-more link-external" href="https://marketplace.guidewire.com/product/canada-nonowned-auto-product-model-by-cognizant/01tPW00000BuW8YYAV">Official listing<span class="visually-hidden"> (opens external site)</span></a>
      </li>
      <li class="card card--listing">
        <h4>Canada Contingent Lessor Product Model</h4>
        <p>A product model for contingent lessor auto insurance in Canada. It covers third-party liability with limits and deductibles for the Canadian provinces, including a Quebec-specific liability coverage, mandatory and additional coverages, coverage terms, and a framework for group-level data elements.</p>
        <p class="works">Works with PolicyCenter and Product Models (APD)</p>
        <a class="link-more link-external" href="https://marketplace.guidewire.com/product/canada-contingent-lessor-product-model-by-cognizant/01tPW00000DuIsPYAV">Official listing<span class="visually-hidden"> (opens external site)</span></a>
      </li>
    </ul>
    <h3 class="group-label">Specialty and professional lines</h3>

        <h4>USA Architects &amp; Engineers Product Model</h4>
        <p>A product model for professional liability insurance for architects and engineers in the United States, with offerings such as alleged or actual negligence, misinterpretations, code violations, and tail coverage for past services, plus mandatory and additional coverages, coverage terms, and question sets.</p>

        <h4>USA Technology Consultants Policy Product Model</h4>
        <p>A product model for professional liability insurance for technology consultants in the United States, with offerings such as technology errors and omissions, misinterpretation, and foreign liability, plus mandatory and additional coverages, coverage terms, and question sets.</p>
        <p class="works">Works with PolicyCenter and Product Models (APD)</p>
        <a class="link-more link-external" href="https://marketplace.guidewire.com/product/usa-technology-consultants-policy-product-model-by-cognizant/01tPW00000CKIY9YAP">Official listing<span class="visually-hidden"> (opens external site)</span></a>

It includes mandatory and optional coverages with limit options, deductibles, exclusions, and a framework for line-level and risk-level data elements. The listing describes its use for aviation policies in North America.</p>

    <h2 id="acc-tools">Platform and data tools</h2>
    <ul class="card-grid card-grid--2">
      <li class="card card--listing">
        <h3>GWCP Intelligent Deployment Orchestrator IG App</h3>
        <p>An Integration Gateway app for Guidewire Cloud Platform that automates deployment readiness checks, validates the latest build, creates database backups before deployment, and runs deployments on a schedule or on demand.</p>
        <p class="works">Listed as working with BillingCenter, ClaimCenter, ContactManager, and PolicyCenter</p>
        <a class="link-more link-external" href="https://marketplace.guidewire.com/product/gwcp-intelligent-deployment-orchestrator-ig-app-by-cognizant/01tPW00000JjqFdYAJ">Official listing<span class="visually-hidden"> (opens external site)</span></a>
      </li>
      <li class="card card--listing">
        <h3>Cloud Data Access (CDA) Ingestion Framework</h3>
        <p>A configurable framework that moves data from the Guidewire Cloud Data Access (CDA) storage bucket into an insurer's downstream data layer, using a two-layer (raw and merged) design with deduplication and support for schema changes.</p>
        <p class="works">Listed as working with BillingCenter, ClaimCenter, Cloud Data Access, ContactManager, and PolicyCenter</p>
